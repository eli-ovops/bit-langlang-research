# Serenity Hard STOP Gates

Each gate must be checked before scoring. A triggered gate blocks, caps, or
penalizes the research status depending on severity.

## STOP-REV0

- definition: The company has no material operating revenue tied to the claimed
  business or thesis area.
- evidence needed to clear: Audited or filed revenue disclosure, segment
  context, and evidence that revenue is tied to operating activity.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate cannot advance beyond caution or blocked
  research status.

## STOP-DEMO-NOT-PRODUCTION

- definition: The thesis relies on demos, samples, evaluations, pilots, or MoUs
  rather than production revenue.
- evidence needed to clear: Production shipments, recognized revenue, repeat
  orders, commercial contracts, or filing-supported production disclosure.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate cannot receive high evidence status.

## STOP-CUSTOMER-UNCLEAR

- definition: Customer relationship, commercial relevance, or economic buyer is
  unclear or over-inferred.
- evidence needed to clear: Customer concentration disclosures, named production
  customer evidence where available, or clear end-market revenue linkage.
- consequence if triggered: Evidence score is capped and R5 readiness is blocked.

## STOP-DILUTION

- definition: Shareholder dilution overwhelms or may overwhelm operating
  progress.
- evidence needed to clear: Share count history, financing terms, cash runway,
  operating cash flow, and dilution disclosures.
- consequence if triggered: Severe survivability and shareholder-capture penalty.

## STOP-SOCIAL-HYPE

- definition: Positive case relies on social-media heat, message-board
  promotion, or narrative crowding.
- evidence needed to clear: Filing-backed technical, customer, production, and
  financial evidence independent of social attention.
- consequence if triggered: Treat social attention as crowding risk only.

## STOP-CUSTOMER-CONCENTRATION

- definition: Revenue depends on too few customers, one program, or fragile
  renewal assumptions.
- evidence needed to clear: Customer concentration disclosure, program duration,
  repeat-order evidence, and revenue diversification.
- consequence if triggered: Durability and evidence scores are capped.

## STOP-CHINA-GEO

- definition: China, geopolitical, sanction, export-control, supply-chain, or
  listing exposure creates material risk.
- evidence needed to clear: Geographic revenue, manufacturing footprint, export
  control, sanction, ownership, and risk-factor disclosures.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate is penalized or blocked if exposure is not
  controllable.

## STOP-VALUATION-OVERHEAT

- definition: Market expectations appear detached from current evidence and
  shareholder capture.
- evidence needed to clear: Risk-only valuation context, peer comparison,
  revenue quality, cash-flow support, and crowding review without target price.
- consequence if triggered: Research priority is capped until evidence catches
  up.

## STOP-GOING-CONCERN

- definition: Filings disclose going-concern doubt or liquidity stress that
  threatens thesis survival.
- evidence needed to clear: Audit opinion, liquidity notes, covenant status,
  cash runway, and financing disclosures.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate is blocked until resolved by public
  filings.

## STOP-SHAREHOLDER-NOT-CAPTURED

- definition: The industry chokepoint may be real but economics do not accrue to
  common shareholders.
- evidence needed to clear: Gross margin, operating leverage, free cash flow,
  dilution, debt, governance, and capital allocation evidence.
- consequence if triggered: Final research score is capped or downgraded.

## STOP-GOVERNANCE-ACCOUNTING

- definition: Governance, accounting, audit, restatement, internal-control, or
  related-party concerns impair evidence reliability.
- evidence needed to clear: Auditor reports, restatement history, internal
  control disclosures, governance filings, and risk factors.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate is blocked or heavily penalized.

## STOP-LINKAGE-WEAK

- definition: Linkage from company revenue to the claimed thesis is indirect,
  speculative, or unsupported.
- evidence needed to clear: Product revenue, segment disclosure, customer use
  case, production evidence, and credible technical linkage.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate cannot remain in priority status.
