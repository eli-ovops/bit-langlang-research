# Serenity STOP Gate Checklist

Scope: R4-A research-only audit. No buy/sell advice, target price, position sizing, entry timing, or trade plan.

Local input status: NO_LOCAL_SERENITY_INPUT_FILES_FOUND

## STOP-REV0
- definition: Company has no material operating revenue tied to the claimed business line.
- evidence required to clear: Audited filings showing operating revenue, segment disclosure, and customer/program context.
- consequence if triggered: Candidate cannot advance beyond caution/reject until revenue exists.
- examples from R2 / R3: R2/R3 caution: pre-revenue or narrative-only photonics names must not be upgraded.

## STOP-DEMO-NOT-PRODUCTION
- definition: Evidence is limited to demos, samples, evaluations, pilots, or MoUs rather than production revenue.
- evidence required to clear: Customer shipments, production contracts, recognized revenue, repeat orders, and filing support.
- consequence if triggered: Treat thesis as unproven; cap status at watch/caution.
- examples from R2 / R3: R2/R3 caution: demo/sample/MoU does not equal production revenue.

## STOP-CUSTOMER-UNCLEAR
- definition: Named or economically meaningful customers cannot be verified from filings, releases, or credible public evidence.
- evidence required to clear: Customer concentration table, named production customer when disclosed, or clear end-market revenue evidence.
- consequence if triggered: Do not assign high evidence score.
- examples from R2 / R3: R3 requirement: customer / production validation remains mandatory for R4-A.

## STOP-DILUTION
- definition: Survival depends on repeated equity issuance or shareholder dilution overwhelms operating progress.
- evidence required to clear: Share count history, ATM/convertible filings, cash runway, and operating cash flow evidence.
- consequence if triggered: Apply severe survivability/shareholder capture penalty.
- examples from R2 / R3: R2 false-positive calibration: exciting technology can fail shareholders through dilution.

## STOP-SOCIAL-HYPE
- definition: Positive case relies on social-media attention, message-board promotion, or meme momentum.
- evidence required to clear: Filing-backed production, revenue, customer, and cash-flow evidence independent of social attention.
- consequence if triggered: Hype is not positive evidence and may add penalty.
- examples from R2 / R3: R3 rule: social-media heat is not evidence.

## STOP-CUSTOMER-CONCENTRATION
- definition: Revenue depends on too few customers or one program with fragile renewal/volume assumptions.
- evidence required to clear: 10-K/20-F customer concentration disclosures, contract duration, diversification, backlog quality.
- consequence if triggered: Require benchmark/risk framing or downgrade evidence durability.
- examples from R2 / R3: R4-A optical names require customer concentration audit.

## STOP-CHINA-GEO
- definition: China/geopolitical exposure creates material supply, demand, ownership, sanction, or listing risk.
- evidence required to clear: Geographic revenue, manufacturing footprint, export controls, VIE/ownership and risk factor disclosures.
- consequence if triggered: Apply geopolitical penalty or block if risk is unmanageable.
- examples from R2 / R3: R4-A benchmark AXTI and some global suppliers require explicit China/geopolitical audit.

## STOP-VALUATION-OVERHEAT
- definition: Market expectations appear detached from current production evidence and shareholder capture.
- evidence required to clear: Multiples, growth quality, cash-flow support, dilution/debt context, and peer comparison without target price.
- consequence if triggered: Do not rank as high conviction until evidence catches up.
- examples from R2 / R3: R3 explicitly stopped target prices and trade plans; valuation review is risk-only.

## STOP-GOING-CONCERN
- definition: Filings disclose going-concern doubt or liquidity stress that threatens research thesis survival.
- evidence required to clear: Audit opinion, liquidity note, debt covenant status, cash runway, financing terms.
- consequence if triggered: Block advancement unless resolved by public filings.
- examples from R2 / R3: R2 false-positive calibration: solvency risk can dominate technology narrative.

## STOP-SHAREHOLDER-NOT-CAPTURED
- definition: The chokepoint may be real but economics accrue to customers, suppliers, creditors, insiders, or capex rather than common shareholders.
- evidence required to clear: Gross margin, operating leverage, FCF conversion, debt/dilution, governance, and capital allocation evidence.
- consequence if triggered: Reduce final research score even if opportunity is real.
- examples from R2 / R3: R4-A must separate industry bottleneck from shareholder capture.

## STOP-GOVERNANCE-ACCOUNTING
- definition: Accounting, disclosure, related-party, restatement, auditor, or governance issues impair trust in evidence.
- evidence required to clear: Auditor reports, restatement history, internal-control disclosures, governance filings.
- consequence if triggered: Block or penalize until resolved.
- examples from R2 / R3: R2/R3 false-positive controls require evidence quality over narrative.

## STOP-LINKAGE-WEAK
- definition: The link from company revenue to optical/silicon photonics/AI infrastructure chokepoint is indirect, speculative, or unsupported.
- evidence required to clear: Segment/product revenue, customer use cases, filings, production releases, and credible technical linkage.
- consequence if triggered: Do not include in priority ranking except benchmark/caution.
- examples from R2 / R3: POET/LWLG remain caution references; weak linkage cannot be upgraded.
