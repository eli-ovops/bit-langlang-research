# Serenity R4-A Deep-Dive Research Plan

Scope: public-source research-only preparation for optical / silicon photonics / optical manufacturing / test candidates. No buy/sell advice, target price, position sizing, entry timing, or trade plan.

## Priority candidates
### 1. TSEM - Tower Semiconductor Ltd.
- current R3 classification: R4-A priority candidate
- why in R4-A: Specialty foundry exposure that may matter if silicon photonics and optical I/O require credible production-grade manufacturing partners.
- suspected chokepoint: specialty foundry / silicon photonics manufacturing enablement
- required latest filings / releases: latest 10-K/10-Q/20-F/6-K or annual/interim report; latest earnings release; investor presentation only as secondary context.
- revenue quality questions: separate production revenue from demo/sample/MoU; identify segment revenue, backlog/order quality, margin and cash conversion.
- customer / production validation questions: verify disclosed customer concentration, production shipment evidence, repeat-order signs, and whether named relationships are commercial production.
- STOP gate questions: audit all STOP gates in serenity_stop_gate_checklist.md; any triggered gate must cap or block R5 readiness.
- valuation crowding questions: assess expectation risk, multiple crowding, cyclicality, and narrative premium without issuing any target price.
- shareholder capture questions: test whether economics accrue to common shareholders after dilution, debt, capex, margins, governance, and customer power.
- R5 underwriting readiness criteria: recent public evidence is current; production revenue linkage is clear; customer concentration is bounded; no unresolved hard STOP; scorecard fields can be filled with citations.
- R4 questions: Can public filings show silicon photonics or optical I/O production linkage? | Is the linkage direct enough to survive STOP-LINKAGE-WEAK? | Does specialty foundry economics accrue to shareholders?
